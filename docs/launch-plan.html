Can step 5 send my design to the cutter automatically?

Not yet. Step 5 (Order) prepares everything: the cut list, the drawing with every angled cut and drill hole, the best-matched cutters for your postcode, and a ready-written quote email. You attach the two files, send the email, the cutter replies with a price, you pay them directly, and they deliver.

Fully automatic ordering ("press a button, panels arrive") needs a partner cutter who accepts orders from Made to Fit, either through an upload link or an API. That is what the partner emails in phase 3 set up. Your own test order is how you learn what to ask for.

This week

1. Order your own eaves cupboards

  • Open the live app on your phone, choose Under-eaves cupboard, add a photo with a bank card on the wall, and measure on the photo.
  • Width, front height, back height and depth. Type the tape number in, or tap "Matches my tape". Measure in two or three places and use the smallest.
  • On step 5, "Download the order pack" saves two files: the cut list (CSV) and the drawing (DXF).
  • CNC Creations (best for angled eaves panels) and Cutwrights (to compare). Use "Draft email" and attach both files yourself. Ask: can you cut from this DXF, label the parts, price delivered to my postcode, and lead time?
  • The app then moves you to the hardware: "Add all to Amazon basket", or copy the list for Screwfix.
  • Did the parts match the picture manual? Anything mislabelled, missing or hard? Note the real cost against the app's estimate. This becomes your partner pitch and your first case study.

Weeks 2 to 4

3. Line up a cutting partner

  • Use your test order as the example and send the order pack brief (docs/ORDER-PACK.md). Ask for: a price list or upload link so the app can show prices, a referral fee or trade price, labelled parts, a set lead time, and who pays for mistakes.
  • Fill in the blanks in the business plan deck with their real numbers.
Months 2 to 3

4. First customers

  • A tracked route to your partner cutters so referrals are counted. Built once a partner says yes.
  • Needed before taking orders or running ads, including the Amazon Associates disclosure.
  • "Made to Fit" in classes 20, 35 and a narrow 42; not 9. Quick attorney check first.
  • Loft conversion and DIY groups, your own build photos. Ask each customer what nearly stopped them.
After 20 to 50 orders

5. Take the payment yourself

One price at checkout for panels, hardware and delivery; the cutter fulfils at trade price and you keep the margin. Then more cutters by region, a fitter network, the Made to Fit hardware pack and an iPhone room-scanning app.
