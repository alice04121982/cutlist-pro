Made to Fit · logo rethink · Chalk Line palette

Four new logo ideas, and the right typeface for them

The first mark (camera brackets around a dimension line) said "photo app" more than "cupboard", and the bold grotesque made the name feel like any other startup. These four start from the name itself: the word "to" is a measurement, the letter m is already a cupboard, a box can fit snugly under a roof, and timber gets marked with a stencil.

Step 1 · Typeface

"made to fit" in eight typefaces

The name is three short words, so the typeface does most of the work. Lowercase reads friendly and approachable; the best faces have a little character in the a, t and f without becoming cute.

Recommendation

Concept A, "the dimension", in Bricolage Grotesque

It is the only one where the name and the product are the same idea: the word "to" becomes a measurement between "made" and "fit", which is exactly what the app does with your photo. The dimension line with its yellow tag works alone as the app icon, and it reuses the measure tag you will see on every design. Concept B (the m cupboard) is the strongest standalone symbol if you would rather have a classic icon plus name.

Next step once you choose: final drawing as outlined SVG (so it no longer depends on a web font), favicon and app icon set, then the header on the site.
