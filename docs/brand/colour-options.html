Made to Fit · colour exploration

Five colour directions, each taken from the workshop or the home

Every direction is drawn from something real in the job: the hi-vis vest, the builder's chalk line, plaster and paint, the spirit level bubble, the carpenter's pencil. Each one is shown on the same home screen in light and dark, so you compare colour, not layout.

Ease of use rules that every direction follows: one colour is reserved for the main action and nothing else. Measurements get their own colour, because measuring is the product's signature moment. Text passes WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1), and buttons pass 3:1 against the page. The checks under each direction are calculated live from the actual colours.

My pick is Chalk Line, with Plaster as the alternative. The audience is a homeowner who is nervous about doing carpentry. Chalk-line blue says "measured and correct", and the yellow measure tag makes the photo-to-measurement step feel like the hero of the app. If you would rather the brand feel like interiors than tools, Plaster is the one.
