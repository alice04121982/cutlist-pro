Headline fonts close to Bricolage, but your own

Your IVF app uses Bricolage Grotesque and Hanken Grotesk, and Stay Prepared uses Archivo, so all three are out. Each option below is free on Google Fonts and is shown as the logo, the home page headline and a step title from the app, in the Chalk Line colours.

My pick is Funnel Display. It has Bricolage's warmth and slightly quirky letters (look at the f, t and y), but with its own sharper, more engineered feel, which suits a measuring tool. Gabarito is the friendlier, rounder alternative.
