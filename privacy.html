Privacy notice

Last updated 30 October 2026.

What stays on your device

Your photos, measurements, designs and saved projects stay in your browser. They are not uploaded. Your delivery postcode stays on this device too.

The design assistant

If you use "Change it", what you type and the sizes of your design are sent to our server and on to an AI model to work out the change. We do not store them.

The cutter waiting list

If you join the list on the order step, we keep:

  • your email address
  • your postcode area, such as SW, if you entered a postcode (not the full postcode)
  • the type of project, such as an under-stairs cupboard
  • the date you joined and which version of the sign-up wording you agreed to

We use these only to email you when a UK cutting service can take your order pack, and to show cutters how many people in each area are waiting. We never give your email to a cutter without asking you first, and we never sell it.

Our lawful basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

We delete your details when you ask, or 12 months after you joined if we have not arranged a cutter by then.

Who handles it

The site is hosted by Vercel. The waiting list is stored by Supabase in its EU data centre. Both act only on our instructions.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Email [contact email to be added] and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Made to Fit sets no cookies and uses no tracking.
